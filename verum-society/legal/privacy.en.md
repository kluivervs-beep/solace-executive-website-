# Verum Society Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE]

Verum Society is a private members club. We handle your data with care. This policy explains what we collect, why, and what your rights are.

## Who we are
Verum Society, [COMPANY NAME], [ADDRESS], Chamber of Commerce (KvK) [KVK NUMBER]. Privacy contact: [PRIVACY EMAIL]. We are the controller of your data.

## What we process
- **Account data:** name, email address and password. The password is stored encrypted and cannot be read by us.
- **Application:** your answers to the questionnaire (for example what you bring to Verum, interests, expertise, motivation, Instagram).
- **Dietary requirements and allergies (optional):** only if you enter them, so we can take them into account at dinners and tastings. This may be health information. We process it only with your explicit consent, which you give when filling it in.
- **Profile:** profile photo, and your chosen theme and language.
- **App use:** your event RSVPs and your chat messages.
- **Technical data:** a device token to send push notifications, and basic data to keep the app secure and stable.

We do not sell your data and do not use it for advertising or tracking.

## Why we do this
- To review your application and manage your membership (performance of the agreement and our legitimate interest in running a private club).
- To organise events and invite you to them.
- To enable chat between members and prevent abuse.
- To send you notifications about events and your membership. Push notifications are sent only with your permission, and you can turn them off at any time.
- Health data (diet/allergy): based on your explicit consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

## Who can see your data
- **Other members:** your name, profile photo and what you share in your profile and chat. Your application answers are visible only to the Verum team.
- **The team:** the founders have access to member data. The event curator only sees who has RSVPed to an event.
- **Processors** working on our behalf: Supabase (database, sign-in and storage, in the EU), Expo (updates and push notifications), and Apple (distribution and push notifications). Data processing agreements are in place. We do not share your data with others unless required by law.

## How long we keep data
- Member data: for as long as you are a member.
- Rejected or withdrawn applications: deleted within [90] days.
- Messages: as long as your account exists; they are deleted when you delete your account.
- Reports of misconduct: until handled, then at most [12] months.

## Deleting your account and data
You can delete your account yourself at any time in the app: **Profile, Delete account**. Your account, profile, profile photo, application, RSVPs and messages are then permanently deleted. This cannot be undone. Backups are overwritten within [30] days. If it does not work in the app, email [PRIVACY EMAIL].

## Your rights
You may request access to, correction or deletion of your data, object to processing, restrict processing, and receive your data in a common format. You can withdraw consent at any time. Email [PRIVACY EMAIL]; we respond within one month. You may also file a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority (autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl).

## Security
Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Access is restricted by role and enforced in the database.

## Age
Verum Society is only for people aged 18 and over.

## Changes
If we make significant changes to this policy, we will tell you in the app.
